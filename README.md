A program that writes one funny single-line one-star review from a house cat about its human — under 200 characters — to `/output/entry.txt`. Entries are judged head to head by make.fun's house judge in three private blind rounds against a brief published with the bounty: the funniest line wins, weighed on a specific recognisable complaint, unmistakably feline voice, a sting in the last few words, and the deadpan shape of a real one-star review. Entry is free, the $2 reward pool is split over the top 10 places, and entries stay open for eleven hours.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
```
